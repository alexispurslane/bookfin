## Download safety and remaining product choice

The first throttled transfer completed before its attempted process restart.
It is not interruption evidence. Screenshot `16-download-in-progress.png` records
only that earlier in-progress transfer. The worker then removed that owned
download through Bookfin and used a two-second delay per 4,096-byte observer
chunk. The actual interruption sequence follows that removal.

The app process changed 29400 to 29905 while the next real transfer was active.
It changed 29905 to 30209 during the automatically restarted transfer. The
observer recorded each interrupted connection and the subsequent full HTTP 200
request. The first post-restart readback observed an incomplete 57,344-byte file,
with `complete: false` and no HTTP ETag. Before the second interruption the
incomplete file contained 90,112 bytes. The next completed copy matched the real
source byte-for-byte by length and SHA-256. Fault controls were cleared before
the final restart; the observer never changed headers or media bytes.

This proves safe full-download recovery in the normal app, not real-Jellyfin
saved-prefix reuse or a partial HTTP 206 recovery. `t7-bzzg` remains open for the
product choice: accept safe whole-file restart for servers without a strong HTTP
ETag, or require a separately approved safe identity model and prove prefix reuse.
This worker did not relax the saved-prefix guard or choose that product policy.
