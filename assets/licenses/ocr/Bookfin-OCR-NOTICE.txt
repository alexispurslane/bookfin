Bookfin bundles Tesseract4Android 4.9.0, Tesseract 5.5.1, Leptonica 1.85.0, and the tessdata_fast 4.1.0 English model.
This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.
Original engine, model, libpng, Leptonica and IJG notices accompany this acknowledgement.
English is the only supported recognition language. No model download or cloud recognition is used.
