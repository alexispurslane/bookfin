English hyphenation patterns of the hyph-utf8 project
https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen, tag CTAN-2024.12.31
(commit 29e003ea905f6740a0d66e114db9f60b26d07516)

Bookfin's reader hyphenates English text with the patterns and exceptions
of hyph-en-us and hyph-en-gb, as Dart data in
lib/reader/epub/hyphenation_en.dart.
