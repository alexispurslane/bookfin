Bookfin changes: retain city identifiers, names, ASCII names, first-level region
names, English country names, country codes, coordinates and population.
Join region and country names into each city record. Omit redundant ASCII names.
Sort by numeric GeoNames identifier. No coordinates are changed.

This snapshot contains 34,195 populated places across 244 country/territory codes.
GeoNames defines cities15000 as places over 15,000 people or capitals.
Some smaller places and subdivisions are included by GeoNames. This is not a
complete list of towns, villages or neighborhoods. Population figures vary in age.
105 records have no available first-level region. These show city and country.
Equal city/region/country labels also show their GeoNames identifier in Bookfin.

The app searches these bundled names and ASCII spellings, regions, English
country names and two-letter country codes. It does not search all GeoNames
alternate names. No permission, device location or online lookup is used.
