DOWN THE RABBIT-HOLE

Alice was beginning to get very tired of
sitting by her sister on the bank, and of
having nothing to do: once or twice she
had peeped into the book her sister was
reading, but it had no pictures or con-
versations in it, “and what is the use of a
book,” thought Alice “without pictures
or conversations?”
