# Fix three small speech failures that nothing handles

From the boo-vh77 review (B9): 1. The unawaited closure of Crossings.prepare (lib/crossings/crossings.dart) leaves the error of a corrupt EPUB, or of a MOBI over 64 MiB, unhandled. 2. HearingPolicy._moved calls HeardWindows.hearSoon without checking that the place fits its edition, so _request can throw an ArgumentError that nothing handles. 3. HearingPolicy._nextFill scans the whole timeline on the UI isolate for each window, O(n^2) over a run; it sends the places of editions that this phone streams through the speech queue; and its timeline(null) calls evict the one-entry cache of Playback.timeline that the History sheet uses.
