# The PDF reader does not show whether Original page or Reflow text is selected

In the PDF reader, the Original page and Reflow text buttons look the same in both modes, so the reader cannot see which view is on. Seen with the Windfall PDF of tool/showcase_fixture.dart; see docs/screenshots/reader-pdf.png and reader-reflow.png.

## Design

Replace the two text buttons above the PDF page with one icon toggle in the reader's bottom bar. The toggle shows only for a PDF. The EPUB and MOBI bars do not change.

- The toggle follows the theme control: its icon and tooltip name the mode that a tap switches to. While the original page shows, it offers "Reflow text". While reflow shows, it offers "Original page".
- The bar gets the reflow value and reports the requested value through a callback. The reader screen, as the controller, calls `reader.setReflow`.
- The toggle stays on while the page text loads. If the person asks for reflow then, the original page shows until the text arrives, and then the reflowed text shows.
- The viewport loses its `onReflow` callback.
- The recognition status is no longer a row above the page. It is a glass layer (`CoverGlass`) over the page, which shows the open book. While the shown page waits for its recognized text, the layer dims the page and shows "Recognizing English text…", a progress indicator, and Stop waiting. Stop waiting reports `onCancelRecognition`, which now means "stop waiting and show the original page". When the text arrives or the person stops waiting, the dimming layer goes away.
- A quiet glass note under the page keeps the English-only note, the reason that a page has no reliable text, and Retry. It does not dim or cover the page, so a scan's last lines stay visible.
- The bar's buttons lose the 4-point gaps between them, so that the eight PDF controls fit in one row on a phone 411 points wide. The EPUB bar keeps its seven buttons, 4 points closer together. In reflow mode, the reason stays in the reflow message above the page, as before.
- The plan says that the ebook reader gets no glass. The user asked for glass on this layer, so this layer is the exception.
