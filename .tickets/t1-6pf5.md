# Decide whether marks in a scan's margin, such as a pencil page number, are content

Found in tb-z9h5. The user decided that only text lines can be running headers, and that regions never are. The Raven scan has a pencil page number in its top-left margin (pixels 101,61 to 119,94 at 1600 wide), a picture region. It stays in the content box, so the page's content box starts at y = 61 instead of 236 (the engraving). Over a whole scanned book, such marks would widen the side boxes of tb-lnak. Two designs: A keeps every region as content (no content is ever cut; the crop keeps the margin mark). B leaves out a region smaller than one body line height in both directions that lies in the outer 12 percent of the page and repeats its place on 30 percent of the side's body pages (the margin mark is cropped; a real small mark there, such as an ornament, could be cut). This is a user decision.
