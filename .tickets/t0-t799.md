# Read an ebook aloud with on-device speech

Requested by the user on 2026-11-09. A read-aloud feature inside the ebook reader only: the phone turns the book into speech on demand, highlights the spoken sentence and word, moves the reading place along, and keeps small Opus audio of what it spoke beside the download. The audio is never uploaded to Jellyfin. Research: docs/research/on-device-tts.md (boo-vv3b). Its design doc will be docs/plans/read-aloud.md, written by the second ticket of this epic.
