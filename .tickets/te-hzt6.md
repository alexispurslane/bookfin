# Estimate the time left in a PDF

Found in boo-02sx. The time left counts visible characters of EPUB and MOBI sections, which BookPages and the pager give. A PDF page has no counted characters, so the reader shows no time left for a PDF. A PDF could count its text layer per page, or use pages per minute. Decide which, and feed the same ReadingSpeed and TimeLeft path.
