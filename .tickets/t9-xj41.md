# Player controls report events up, and a playing tick rebuilds only the position widgets

Rules: UI rule 1 (only controllers cause side effects). UI rule 2 (one component for each kind of thing). UI rule 3 (subscribe at the lowest level).

Breaks:
- lib/playback/screen.dart, PlayerView.build and PlaybackMiniPlayer.build: a ListenableBuilder on the whole Playback. Each 200 ms tick rebuilds the whole stage and the whole mini player.
- lib/playback/screen.dart, _PlayButton (play, pause), _JumpButton (jump), _PositionSlider (beginSliderSeek, finishSliderSeek) and _UndoControl (undoSeek, dismissUndo): leaves call Playback.
- lib/playback/screen.dart, ReplacementStartNotice: listens to the whole Playback, confirms, and calls confirmReplacement and cancelReplacement.
- lib/playback/screen.dart, PlaybackMiniPlayer: opens the player sheet from an inline InkWell, and its buttons call Playback.
- lib/playback/screen.dart, PlayerView._tools: the Sleep label computes the minutes left from DateTime.now in build, so it changes only when something else rebuilds the stage.
- lib/playback/records_screen.dart, RecordSaveStatus: calls playback.flush, and reads message and saveFailed without subscribing.
- lib/playback/records_screen.dart, _BookmarkEditor: a dialog that calls addBookmark and editBookmark itself.
- lib/playback/recent.dart, _RecentRow: calls playback.open. RecentListeningList reads playback.recent and progress without subscribing.
- UI rule 2: the audio _BookmarkEditor and the reader's AlertDialog in lib/reader/screen.dart, _BookmarksPanel, both edit a bookmark title and note.

Target design:
- PlayerView is the controller of the Listening page and of the player pane.
- PlaybackMiniPlayer, ReplacementStartNotice and RecordSaveStatus keep their constructors. Each is a thin controller entry for a player surface that several screens show (see the design fork below). Each entry wires a pure leaf.
- The leaves get values or narrow listenables and report events: onPlayPause, onJump(forward), onSeekStart, onSeekEnd(milliseconds), onUndo, onDismissUndo, onOpen, onStartReplacement, onRetrySave and onResume(entry).
- The slider, the time texts and the mini player's progress line subscribe to Playback's position signal (boo-y6fb). The Sleep label subscribes to the sleep signal. The stage subscribes to the open book and edition. The tools row subscribes to the settings and the bookmarks.
- One bookmark editor: showBookmarkEditor(context, title, note, place label) returns the edited title and note, or null. The caller saves. The reader screen ticket moves the reader to it.
- Keep editAudioBookmark, openPlayer, RecordSaveStatus and RecentListeningList working for lib/playback/timeline.dart and lib/catalog/book_page.dart.

Decided by the user on November 7, 2026: Option A. The mini player, the replacement notice and the record save status each keep one public widget that acts as the controller for its surface and wraps a pure leaf.

Former design fork:
- Option A (recommended): a surface that appears on several screens, such as the mini player, the replacement notice and the record save status, keeps one public widget that acts as the controller entry for that surface and wires a pure leaf. Cost: three widgets outside the tab and page list cause side effects. Gain: no host repeats the wiring, and the constructors that the shell, the book page, the reader and LibraryServices.page call stay as they are.
- Option B: the surfaces become pure leaves, and each host wires them through helper functions such as startReplacement(context, playback, request), as changeDownload does. Cost: the shell, LibraryServices.page, BookPage, PlayerView and ReaderScreen each wire five to seven callbacks, and this ticket must edit their files, which serializes it with the library, book page and reader tickets.

Also: boo-7al5 (compact Undo notice) changes lib/playback/screen.dart. It now depends on this ticket.

Owned files: lib/playback/screen.dart, lib/playback/records_screen.dart, lib/playback/recent.dart, and their tests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A playing tick rebuilds the slider, the time texts and the mini player's progress line. It does not rebuild the stage, the cover or the tools row (rebuild counters).
- The Sleep label counts down while nothing else changes.
- The play button, the jump buttons, the slider, Undo, the replacement notice and the recent rows report through callbacks. Each leaf test pumps the leaf with no Playback and checks the callback.
- A slider drag still records one seek with its start and end positions (existing test).
- The bookmark editor returns the title and note and saves nothing itself.
