# Give ebooks the same bookmarks, reading history and jumps through history as audiobooks

Requested by the user on 2026-11-09: add bookmarks, location history and jumping between history entries to ebooks, like audiobooks have, reusing as much of the same interface as possible. Today an ebook has reading bookmarks (TextBookmark, the bookmarks tab of the contents sheet, the shared bookmark editor), but no reading history, no history timeline, no Undo after a jump, no quick bookmark tool and no bookmark marks on the scrub bar. The audiobook design is in docs/plans/bookfin.md ("Quick bookmarks, history timeline, and stacked glass", and the personal record store and sync sections). The design doc for this epic is docs/plans/reading-history.md (to be written).
