# Drop the pending move when the pager cannot find the place that a book opens at

When a jump into another ebook edition, or an open, gives the pager an initial place whose quote does not match, the pager shows the book's start, reports no place, and warns. The pending move stays until the next page turn, which ends it at that page. The session can drop the pending move when the open shows no place, without dropping it for unrelated warnings that come before the first place.
