# Keep the side boxes narrow when a plate flips the parity of many sampled pages

Found in tb-lnak. Sides come from the parity of the source page number. A plate inserted in the middle of a scanned book flips the parity of about half the sample, so both side boxes grow to hold both margin layouts. Nothing is cut, but the text of every page shows smaller. A design could assign each sampled body page to the side whose margins it fits, before the percentiles. See docs/plans/pdf-word-image-reflow.md, the known limits after 'The survey'.
