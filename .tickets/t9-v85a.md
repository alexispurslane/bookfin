# Derive the audio outline of an audio edition in one pure function

Step 2 of design A in docs/plans/cross-edition-places.md. audioOutline(Edition) gives the chapters of an audio edition (title, start in milliseconds from the edition's start) and the edition's length, from the catalog alone. Playback.chapters uses it, and Crossings uses it. No change of behavior in the player.
