# Let a scrub land on a full-page image inside a section

Found in boo-ok9i. A page that shows no text, such as a full-page illustration inside a section, has no visible character of its own. Its share of the scrub bar names the next page with text, and a drag lands there, so a scrub never reaches the image page (2 of 265 pages of Standard Ebooks' Alice). A turn still reaches it. A fix needs the seek of a bar target to name a true page, not a text progress.
