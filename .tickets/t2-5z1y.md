# Hear windows while listening, at moves and bookmarks, and when idle and charging

The listening policy of the heard windows: one 8 s window for each 5 minutes of listening, and one at each place that a new listening move, pause or bookmark names. Places that no window covers (old or synced entries) wait for a fill: newest first, only while the phone charges and battery saver is off, and only when the speech worker has nothing else to do. Downloaded audio editions only. Playback must not stutter: measure it.


## Notes

**2026-11-11T23:40:22Z**

Built HearingPolicy (lib/speech/hearing_policy.dart) and wired it in main.dart. Listening: decoder reports that move 0 to 5 s forward in the same file while playing count as listening; at each 5 min the policy asks for the 8 s that end at the place. Live places: each new history entry or bookmark of the open edition that is under 60 s old at its arrival; a move also names its 'before' place. Older or synced records wait for the fill. Fill: the newest timeline place that no window covers, one at a time at the lowest queue priority, while the Android power channel (bookfin/power: on power and battery saver off) allows it; it starts again when that turns on. A place that heard nothing is not tried again in the run. Platform split lib/speech/platform*.dart keeps dart:ffi out of web builds; only Android creates the speech runtime. Tests: test/speech/hearing_policy_test.dart (11). Playback stutter is measured in t2-4ci0.

**2026-11-12T01:28:14Z**

Review fix (B4): the power receiver of the bookfin/power stream now belongs to the application (PowerEvents in MainActivity.kt), because Dart listens once for the life of the engine, which outlives an Activity under audio_service. Before, the Activity's onDestroy unregistered it, and the fill could go on after unplugging. Checked by building the debug APK.
