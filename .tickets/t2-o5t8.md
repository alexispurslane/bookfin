# Hear again a place in the second half of a kept window

HeardWindow.covers accepts a place anywhere in a window. On the emulator (t2-4ci0), whisper sometimes gave every word after about 4 s the same time, 7,990 ms. A bookmark 3 s after a kept window's middle is then not heard again, and landHeard picks the first word with the collapsed time, about 3 s early. Decide when a kept window covers a place, for example only in its first 5 s, or only where its word times still grow, and test it with collapsed times. Found in the boo-vh77 review (B8).
