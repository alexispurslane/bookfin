# Run record-store-driving widget tests in real time, then lower the record store hop limits

From t6-n8y3. lib/playback/store.dart keeps _hopItems = 16 and _hopText = 4 KiB as a compromise, because widget tests run in fake time, in which an isolate hop never completes. Its own comment says that a hop pays from about four actions or eight records. t6-n8y3 added test/real_time.dart (finish and runUntil), which runs real and fake time in turns until work ends. Drive the record-store operations that widget tests await through that helper, measure the break-even point in AOT, and lower both limits to it.

## Acceptance Criteria

Every widget test that awaits a record-store operation passes with the record store limits at the measured break-even point, and a test locks in the new limits through behavior.


## Notes

**2026-11-08T02:41:03Z**

Measured break-even, Nov 7 (tool/research/record_hop_bench, AOT, medians of 201 runs, time on the calling isolate): a hop costs 9 to 11 µs. Restoring an account from rows costs 9 µs with no action and 27 µs with one; the views of one record cost 12 µs and of none 6 µs; a saved text without records costs as much as a hop at about 32 characters. Limits: _hopItems = 1 and _hopText = 32. record_hop_test locks them in: one saved place and a one-record text hop, an account with only settings and a 15-character text do not. test/durability/background_test.dart now waits for the saves that a lifecycle change starts through finish, by killing and restarting until the place shows, so it still fails when hiding the app saves nothing. The rest of the suite passed unchanged at these limits.
