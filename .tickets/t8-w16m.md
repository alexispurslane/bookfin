# Decide how a scanned picture's paper shows in the word view

In the word view, a picture region keeps its colors as the design says: on cream it multiplies by the paper, on night it shows at 80 percent. A scanned picture, such as the Raven's engraving, then keeps its scan's darker paper inside the theme's paper, a darker box around the picture. The alternative divides its colors by the page's paper lightness (PageAnalysis.paperLightness), so the scan's paper becomes the theme's paper, at the cost of brighter and more saturated colors in the picture. A user decision. Found in tb-xjfi; previews in ~/.cache/bookfin/previews/tb-xjfi/raven-cream.png and raven-night.png.
