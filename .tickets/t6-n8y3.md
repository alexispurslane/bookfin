# Run catalog-driving widget tests in real time, then lower the catalog hop limits

From t9-se8f. A widget test runs in fake time, and in fake time an isolate hop never completes (tested: Isolate.run and compute time out inside testWidgets, also from Zone.root). Many widget tests await catalog.connect, refresh, restore and corrections in the test body, for example test/catalog/screen_test.dart, test/catalog/book_page_test.dart and test/reader/catalog_flow_test.dart. So lib/catalog/controller.dart builds catalogs below _hopEntries (16 entries) and _hopText (16 KiB) on the UI isolate. Measured in AOT, a hop pays from one or two entries with descriptions. Target: drive those calls through tester.runAsync, or through a helper such as finish in test/catalog/shell_rebuild_test.dart, then lower both limits to the measured break-even point.

## Acceptance Criteria

Every widget test that awaits a catalog operation passes with _hopEntries = 2 and _hopText = 4 KiB.
