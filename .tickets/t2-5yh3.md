# Start and cancel the speech work of a History entry by named operations

Today Crossings.landingNow starts the speech work of a place as a side effect of a query, and openHistory calls it while the timeline builds its rows. The work runs at the waiting priority with 4 threads and is never cancelled: after Cancel, after the entry collapses, or after the sheet closes, its windows still go ahead of the listening windows, and a reading place can queue 6 of them (36.9 s on the emulator, t2-4ci0). Give the controller named operations, such as 'this entry opened' and 'this entry or the sheet closed', so that only an open entry, or a Go to that waits, holds urgent windows, and the rest drop to the listening priority or leave the queue. Found in the boo-vh77 review (B7, D3).
