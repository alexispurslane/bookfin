# Lay out a long verse paragraph in steps of its hard-broken segments

Found in t1-qtde. After t1-qtde, the layout step of a paragraph with thousands of lines is linear in its lines, but one engine call still shapes the whole paragraph. Song of Myself in Gutenberg's Leaves of Grass (section 27, item 9: one pre element of 91,000 characters) takes 96 ms in one step at 412 x 671 at 20 pixels and 143 ms at 360 x 640 at 26 pixels on the host (JIT, light load). Paragraph.layout alone takes 69 ms and 120 ms of that. The phone is probably 1.5 to 2.5 times slower, so this step can freeze the reader for a few frames. Line breaking never crosses a hard break, so a paragraph can be built and laid out as several engine paragraphs of whole hard-broken segments with the same lines, and a step can stop between them. The orphans and widows rules, the hanging first line, placeholders and the line starts of LayoutRun._place must keep their results.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No layout step of Song of Myself takes more than about the budget plus one segment, at both corpus page settings, by the corpus slow-item list or the on-demand benchmark test/reader/epub/step_budget_test.dart.
- The lines, pages and places of every paragraph stay the same (fidelity test, saved places test, corpus runs of Leaves of Grass).


## Notes

**2026-11-09T13:56:05Z**

Built: a long paragraph with hard breaks lays out in segments of whole lines that end after a hard break, at least 1,024 code units each (LayoutRun._segmentsOf, _segment). Each segment is a unit of work and a piece of its own; a line waits until the lines after it decide its widows rule. The lines before each segment are counted once per paragraph item, so runs from page starts and LineSearch lay out only the segment that holds their line or place, and LineSearch counts the segments before it in steps. Song of Myself: slowest unit 79 and 122 ms before, under 4 ms after at both settings; Origin of Species index 23 and 19 ms before, 6 and under 4 ms after. A page dump of 47 corpus books at both settings is identical before and after (4,822 section layouts).
