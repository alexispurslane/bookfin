# Measure the layout cost of hyphenation on the Pixel 8

tc-z7au hyphenates text that asks for hyphens: auto (all Standard Ebooks books). On the host, whole-book layout of three Standard Ebooks books takes 2.4 to 2.6 times as long with hyphenation, because a paragraph with a hyphenated line lays out twice, and prepare takes about twice as long (docs/plans/native-epub-reader.md, Hyphenation). Measure the time to the first page and a page turn of a Standard Ebooks book on the Pixel 8 with and without hyphenation. If the first page or a turn misses its budget, find a cheaper way to place hyphens (for example a drawn hyphen for lines that are not justified).

## Acceptance Criteria

The report gives the phone's first-page and page-turn times for a Standard Ebooks book with and without hyphenation, and says whether they meet the step budget.
