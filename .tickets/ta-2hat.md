# Keep the bookmark editor's place line clear of the title field's label

Found in the emulator check of t9-7fxw (docs/evidence/reader/dock-history/03-edit-bookmark.png) and also visible in docs/evidence/reader/history-timeline/03-rename-reading-bookmark.png. In the shared glass bookmark editor, the place line, such as 'On p. 1 · One. The Grafted Trees', sits so close above the outlined Title field that the field's floating label 'Title (optional)' overlaps the bottom of the place line's letters. Seen on a Pixel 7 profile at the default text size.

## Acceptance Criteria

The place line and the floating label of the Title field do not overlap, at the default and the largest system text size, in the reader and in the player.
