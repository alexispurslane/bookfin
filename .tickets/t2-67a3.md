# Decode a window of a downloaded audiobook to 16 kHz speech samples

A native decoder in native/speech: Android NDK AMediaExtractor and AMediaCodec, macOS ExtAudioFile. It gives the mono 16 kHz float samples of a window of a downloaded MP3, M4B or M4A file, clamped to the file. No platform channel, no UI isolate work.


## Notes

**2026-11-11T23:00:03Z**

Built: bs_decode in native/speech/bookfin_speech.c. macOS: ExtAudioFile converts to 16 kHz mono float. Android: NDK AMediaExtractor + AMediaCodec from the sync point before the window, mono mix, linear resampling (as the prototype). Linux host: no decoder yet (returns -1). test/speech/decoder_test.dart: MP3 and M4A windows, seek accuracy, end of file, missing file. The Android path compiles; it runs on the emulator in t2-4ci0.
