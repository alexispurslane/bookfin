# Decode a window of a downloaded audiobook to 16 kHz speech samples

A native decoder in native/speech: Android NDK AMediaExtractor and AMediaCodec, macOS ExtAudioFile. It gives the mono 16 kHz float samples of a window of a downloaded MP3, M4B or M4A file, clamped to the file. No platform channel, no UI isolate work.
