# Measure PDF search time on books whose text layers are often out of order

Found in boo-981b on 2026-11-09. Search now uses the same text for reading as reflow. A page whose text layer is out of order (multi-column pages, tables, damaged font maps) is recognized during search, as a scanned page already was. Before boo-981b, search skipped such pages and said so. On a text PDF with many such pages, a search can now take seconds for each of them. Measure on Android with a two-column paper and a table-heavy book, and decide whether search needs a cheaper path.

## Acceptance Criteria

A measured search time on Android for both books, and a recorded decision.
