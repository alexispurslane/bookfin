# The Downloads tab confirms and runs download changes, and each row follows one edition

Rules: UI rule 1 (only controllers cause side effects, no long-lived data in a leaf State). UI rule 2 (no dead duplicates). UI rule 3 (subscribe at the lowest level).

Breaks:
- lib/downloads/screen.dart, DownloadsPage: the shell rebuilds it on each Downloads byte notice, and each build rescans every book and edition.
- lib/downloads/screen.dart, _Summary: confirms mobile data and calls policy.allowMobileForSession and policy.resetSession.
- lib/downloads/screen.dart, _LeftoverInventory: owns the retainedStorage future in a leaf State.
- lib/downloads/screen.dart, _LeftoverSummary: pushes LeftoverFilesPage from a leaf.
- lib/downloads/screen.dart, DownloadControls, DownloadButton and _DownloadRow: read downloads.status(edition) and depend on a parent rebuild instead of subscribing to their edition.
- lib/downloads/screen.dart, bookAvailability: no caller. Delete it.

Target design:
- DownloadsTab is the controller of the tab. It owns the confirmations, the mobile-data choice, navigation to LeftoverFilesPage, and changeDownload. _Summary reports onAllowMobile and onWifiOnly. _LeftoverSummary reports onOpenLeftovers and onRetry.
- The tab subscribes to the catalog's library listenable, to the layout density, and to Downloads.phases for the list of editions. Each row subscribes to Downloads.watch(edition), so byte progress rebuilds only that row.
- DownloadControls and DownloadButton keep their constructors (downloads, edition, onAction) and subscribe to watch(edition) themselves. The book page uses them unchanged.
- The leftover summary and LeftoverFilesPage read Downloads.leftovers. LeftoverFilesPage stays the controller of its page and calls reloadLeftovers and removeRetained.

Owned files: lib/downloads/screen.dart and its tests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A byte notice rebuilds only the row of that edition. The summary and the other rows do not rebuild.
- The trash icon reports onAction(remove). The tab confirms, then calls Downloads.remove. A fake proves that nothing is removed before the confirmation.
- The mobile-data button reports up. The tab confirms, then calls allowMobileForSession.
- Opening the Downloads tab twice takes the inventory from Downloads.leftovers and reads storage no extra time (a counting fake).
- DownloadButton rebuilds on its edition's watch notice without a rebuild of its parent.
