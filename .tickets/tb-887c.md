# Decide whether Bookfin state may occupy ordinary private playlists

t3-7fv1 measured that owner-private playlist payloads are confidential on GET /Items/{id}, but ordinary owner rename overwrites payload, delete removes it despite content-deletion being disabled, and an explicit public toggle exposes it to another ordinary user. Unfiltered playlist queries include serialized payload names. A normal library scan and clean restart preserve them. App ownership is a naming convention, not an app-only permission boundary.

## Design

docs/research/jellyfin-playlist-protocol.md

## Acceptance Criteria

Parent obtains a decision on accepting visible app-data playlists and the need not to rename, share, or delete them, versus requiring a namespace separate from ordinary playlist management. Consider immutable batch/checkpoint objects rather than rejecting all protocols from one-object-per-event clutter. Do not select or install a production plugin; its lower-risk claim remains unproved.
