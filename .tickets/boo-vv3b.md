# Research the best on-device text-to-speech model for reading an ebook aloud

Requested by the user on 2026-11-09. A read-aloud feature that lives only inside the ebook reader: the phone converts the book to speech on demand as it reads, highlights what it reads, and moves the reading progress along. It saves small compressed audio of what it converted, on the phone only, for later playback; it never uploads that audio to Jellyfin. Start from Kokoro and find whether better models that run on a phone exist now. Compare quality, speed on the Pixel 8, size, license, runtime from Flutter, word or sentence timing for highlighting, long-form prosody, and storage per book. Recommend a model and a design, and record the work as tickets. No product code.
