# Read aloud for hours with the screen off: background, interruptions and heat

Keep speech and synthesis running in the mediaPlayback foreground service with the screen off. Save the spoken place every 30 s in the background. Pause on calls and other audio. Use fewer threads at a moderate or worse thermal status. Apply the sleep timer. Stop when the person leaves the book. Test a kill mid-segment and a kill mid-save.
