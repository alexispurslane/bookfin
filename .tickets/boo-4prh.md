# Make floating layers glass panes tinted by their book

Sheets, dialogs, menus and snackbars outside the reader become CoverGlass panes. The tint is the book that owns the surface, else the playing book, else plain sepia frost. Pages stay paper. Decided November 7, 2026; see docs/plans/bookfin.md.
